For faculty: how this replaces the long form
Music Tech Den remains the entry point. This flow gathers coursework, identifies the normal or equivalent-competency route, shows course-specific modules, and gathers only relevant questions. A future version could send the request to the Director’s form or review system.
The readiness preview would be informal. An instructor’s Week 1 check would confirm skills. Passing criteria, deadlines, tutoring, completion records, and consequences still need review. MUS 244 remains required where the curriculum requires it.
