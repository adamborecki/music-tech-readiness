04 / PERMISSION TO READINESS

One process.
Two distinct decisions.

Admission happens first.
Readiness is confirmed in Week 1.

  1. 01

    Start in Music Tech Den

    Read the course profile and request a permission code through the Director’s Google Form.

  2. 02

    Show your starting point

    List completed prerequisites. If a prerequisite is missing, try the informal readiness preview and describe relevant experience.

  3. 03

    Review & approval

    The Director reviews exceptions. Proposed equivalent-competency approval is provisional; a self-check never issues a code.

  4. 04

    Confirm in Week 1

    Complete the instructor’s required checks. Review-only areas and must-pass skills are specified per course.

Faculty specification: support, records, and unresolved rules

Proposed: students who completed the prerequisite but are rusty receive targeted review, tutoring, and a retest. Students provisionally admitted without it may need to leave the course if required readiness is not demonstrated. Consequences, deadlines, and institutional procedures are not finalized.

Completion is intended to be reusable within a semester, with refreshers in later semesters. The Director would likely own the completion record; the actual record system is undecided.

Confirm approval authority, requirements in the current catalog, assessment standards, tutor capacity, and add/drop timing before publishing an operational policy. The informal readiness preview and an administered diagnostic examination remain distinct.

06 / SAY THE SAME THING

A small, shared vocabulary.

“Music Tech Readiness” is the umbrella.
“Power up” is the invitation.

Course Readiness Profile
The course’s starting expectations: shared skills, software, and participation commitments.
Readiness Module
A reusable piece of learning or review, paired with a way to demonstrate the relevant skill.
Readiness Preview
An informal self-check to help students judge their preparation. It is not verified evidence of competency.
Equivalent Competency
The proposed basis for approving access without the listed prerequisite. It does not award prerequisite-course credit.
Week 1 Readiness Check
An instructor’s confirmation of the skills the class will assume after the first week.
Course-Fit Acknowledgment
Agreement to participation expectations, such as MUS 324’s acoustic recording and peer listening.
